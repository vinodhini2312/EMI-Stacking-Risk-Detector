💡 Why EMI Stacking Occurs

When users take loans from different apps and banks (e.g. Personal Loan, Phone EMI, Amazon Pay Later), each lender picks a due date (often 5th, 6th, or 10th of the month).

Individually, each EMI might seem affordable (e.g. ₹4,500). But when 3 payments collide in the same week, the total obligation jumps to ₹14,200, exceeding weekly cashflow.

✅ Solution: Use our Simulator to stagger due dates or choose repayment days right after your primary paycheck arrives.
